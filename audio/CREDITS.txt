Changes in this application: playback onset trim, pitch interpolation for missing note, velocity gain/filter, strum timing, envelopes and convolution ambience.
Muted/percussive articulation is derived from these guitar samples, not a separately recorded guitar slap.

Revision 3: gentle spectral denoising (8 dB), 45 Hz high-pass and 6.5 kHz low-pass; samples limited to 6 seconds. Original recordings remain in source history.
